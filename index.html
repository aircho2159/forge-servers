COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reserve

How much does a server cost?

The 4 GB Starter plan is $5 per month. The 8 GB DDR3 Standard plan is $10 per month.

How many servers are available?

There are currently three servers: two 4 GB Starter servers and one 8 GB DDR3 Standard server.

How many players are recommended?

The 4 GB Starter server allows a maximum of 10 players, but up to 5 is recommended for fast gameplay. The 8 GB Standard server allows a maximum of 25 players, but up to 20 is recommended for fast gameplay.

Can I use mods or plugins?

Yes. There is no restriction against using mods on the 4 GB or 8 GB servers, but modded gameplay is not recommended on either current plan. A 12 GB server intended for mods is coming soon.

Can I upload an existing world?

Potentially. Share the world size and relevant information in the ticket so Forge Staff can review the request.

How do I pay?

The payment method has not been permanently selected. Approved customers receive manual payment instructions after availability and compatibility are confirmed.

Are backups included?

Backups are available for every Starter and Standard server. Confirm the backup schedule and restoration process in your Discord ticket.
