STARTER POLICIES

Simple rules for a fair launch

These are practical beta policies for the first Forge Servers customer and can be revised as the service grows.

Service Terms

  • The beta plan costs $5 per month and is paid in advance.
  • Service begins after payment is confirmed and setup details are received.
  • Final performance depends on the server hardware, Minecraft version, mods, plugins, and player load.
  • Forge Servers may schedule maintenance when required.

Cancellation & Refunds

  • You may cancel before the next billing date.
  • Cash App customers must manually renew each month.
  • A refund may be issued if Forge Servers cannot provide the paid service.
  • No refund is promised after setup and access have been delivered, except for unresolved service failure.

Acceptable Use

  • No malware, botnets, crypto mining, attacks, fraud, or illegal content.
  • No attempts to access the host machine, another customer, or private infrastructure.
  • No deliberate network abuse, denial-of-service activity, or exploit distribution.
  • Serious violations may result in immediate suspension.

Privacy

  • Only information needed for payment, setup, and support should be collected.
  • Never send card numbers, passwords, or banking information through Discord.
  • Payment details are handled by the selected payment provider, not this website.
  • Customer information should not be sold or publicly shared.

Starter policy draft—not legal advice. Review it before accepting customers and update it as the business changes.
